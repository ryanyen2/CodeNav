1. Summary

The survey covered four hundred kilometres of shoreline between March
and September. Rates of retreat were higher than the 2019 baseline at
every site except Ardmore, where a new revetment has held the line.

Measurements were taken at low water on each visit, so the figures
below are comparable between sites and between years.


1Coastal Erosion Survey 2026            Marine Institute

2. Method

A fixed-wing drone flew each site at ninety metres. Ground control
points were re-surveyed at the start of each visit, because settle-
ment had moved two of the 2019 markers.

Cliff top position was digitised from the orthophoto at 1:500, which
is the scale the 2019 survey used.


2Coastal Erosion Survey 2026            Marine Institute

3. Results

Ardmore retreated 0.1 metres per year, which is within measurement
error of no change at all. The revetment appears to be holding for
now.

Every other site retreated faster than in 2019. Cloghane lost 1.8
metres, the largest single figure in the series.
