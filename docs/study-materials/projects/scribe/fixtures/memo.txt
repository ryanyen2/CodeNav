Note on the ﬁling backlog

The backlog stands at about three weeks. It has been growing since
the ﬁrst week of June, when two of the four clerks moved to the new
intake team.

The intake team's own note put it plainly:

    Nothing here is slow. Everything here waits.

I do not think this is a stafﬁng problem so much as a sequencing one.
Files are opened in the order they arrive, and a ﬁle that needs a
second signature goes back to the bottom of the pile each time it
moves. A ﬁle needing three signatures can wait a month for work that
takes twenty minutes.What I would try ﬁrst is a separate queue for anything already part-
signed, worked before new arrivals. That costs nothing and would, I
think, clear most of the tail.

If that does not work, the honest answer is that we need a fourth
clerk back.
